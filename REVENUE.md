# 💰 How Your Website Will Start Earning Money

## 🚀 Immediate Revenue Streams (Week 1-2)

### 1. **Google AdSense** - Your Primary Income Source
**Setup Time**: 1-2 days  
**First Payment**: 30-45 days  
**Expected Revenue**: $50-500/month initially

#### How to Start:
1. **Apply for AdSense** at [adsense.google.com](https://adsense.google.com)
2. **Get Approved** (usually takes 1-2 weeks)
3. **Add Your Publisher ID** to `.env.local`:
   ```env
   VITE_ADSENSE_CLIENT=ca-pub-xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
   ```
4. **Create Ad Units** in AdSense dashboard:
   - Top Banner (728x90)
   - Sticky Sidebar (160x600)
   - In-Article ads (300x250)
   - Home page ads (728x90)

#### Revenue Timeline:
- **Week 1**: $5-20 (first ads start showing)
- **Month 1**: $50-200 (steady traffic)
- **Month 3**: $200-800 (optimized placement)

### 2. **Affiliate Marketing** - Commission on Every Sale
**Setup Time**: 1-3 days  
**First Commission**: 30-90 days (when customers buy)  
**Expected Revenue**: $100-1000/month

#### Quick Start Affiliate Programs:
1. **Amazon Associates** (4% commission)
   - Sign up at [affiliate-program.amazon.com](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com)
   - Replace placeholder URLs in `src/pages/Index.tsx`
   - Promote AI tools, books, gadgets

2. **ClickBank** (up to 75% commission)
   - Join at [clickbank.com](https://clickbank.com)
   - Find AI/tech products with high gravity
   - Update affiliate links

3. **CJ Affiliate** (varies by product)
   - Apply at [cj.com](https://cj.com)
   - Focus on software and tech products

#### Smart Affiliate Features:
- **Auto-location detection** routes users to best-paying offers
- **Device optimization** for mobile/desktop
- **Real-time tracking** of clicks and conversions
