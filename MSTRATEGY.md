💰 The Ultimate Web Monetization Funnel
Step 1 — The First Visit: Capture and Monetize Immediately

When someone lands on your site:

Google AdSense / Display Ads → Immediate revenue from impressions/clicks

Affiliate Offer in-content → e.g., “Recommended tools/products”

Pop-up Email/Push Capture → “Get free updates / exclusive content”

Step 2 — Email + Push Notifications

Once they subscribe or allow notifications:

Send daily content links → More ad views

Include affiliate links in newsletters

Special offers once a week for products/services in your niche

Example:

“Here’s today’s top 5 tools you need — [Read Here]” (article has ads + affiliate links)

Step 3 — Upsell / Premium Offer

For loyal visitors:

Membership plan ($2–$5/month) for exclusive content/resources

E-books, guides, or templates for a one-time payment

Webinars or online training in your niche

Step 4 — Sponsor Deals

Once your site is hitting 300k visitors/month:

Offer sponsored posts at $50–$200 each

Banner placements for companies

Email newsletter sponsorship ($100–$500 per send)

Step 5 — Retarget & Keep Visitors Coming Back

Facebook Pixel + Google Ads retargeting

Show ads to past visitors to bring them back

More visits = more ad clicks = more affiliate sales
